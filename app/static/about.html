About

The people behind Boxology Studio.

Boxology Studio pairs three AI assistants — Scholar, Boxologist, and Cartographer — with a live SPARQL-queryable knowledge graph and an in-app diagram editor, so you can explore the literature, design a neuro-symbolic architecture, and follow the evidence behind it in one place. It builds on Tool4Boxology, the reference editor for the Boxology notation.

What's inside

Three specialists, one workspace.

S

Scholar

Explore your paper collection with answers grounded in sources -- every claim comes with the literature it was drawn from.

B

Boxologist

Discuss a system design in plain language, then generate a valid Boxology diagram -- ready to rearrange in-app or export to Tool4Boxology.

C

Cartographer

Query the project's knowledge graph directly with SPARQL, or just ask a question and watch it link, read, rank, and walk the graph for you.
