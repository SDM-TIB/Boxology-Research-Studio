Getting started

Boxology Studio pairs three AI assistants with a live knowledge graph and an in-app diagram preview. Here's how a typical project flows.

1

Describe the system

Open Boxologist and describe your goal, the data you have, the models you need, and any rules or constraints.

→
2

Discuss & refine

Talk through the design in plain language. Boxologist asks clarifying questions and proposes building blocks.

Generate the diagram

Ask it to build the diagram. A valid Tool4Boxology .boxology file is produced and previewed live.

4

Review & export
